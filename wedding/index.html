Travel & staying over

Useful information for guests travelling to Malvern

Parking

The best parking for Great Malvern Priory is at Waitrose, where free parking is available for up to three hours.

The reception hall has its own car park with ample parking.

From London

Great Western Railway (GWR) runs a good service from London Paddington to Great Malvern.

Staying overnight

The Great Malvern Hotel is reasonably priced and within walking distance of Great Malvern Priory.

There are lots of nice places for breakfast in the town.
