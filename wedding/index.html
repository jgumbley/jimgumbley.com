Travel & staying over

Useful information for guests travelling to Malvern

Parking

The best parking for Great Malvern Priory is at Waitrose, where free parking is available for up to three hours.

The reception hall has its own car park with ample parking.

  • Find Waitrose Great Malvern

Arriving by rail

Great Western Railway (GWR) runs a good service from London Paddington to Great Malvern.

  • Great Malvern station information

Staying overnight

The Great Malvern Hotel is reasonably priced and within walking distance of Great Malvern Priory.

There are lots of nice places for breakfast in the town.

  • Visit the Great Malvern Hotel
  • Browse local cafés

Taxis

Uber generally works well in Malvern, although pick-up times can be longer than in larger towns and cities.

If you would rather use a local firm for the 22:30 carriages, options include Smith Taxis, Access Taxis and BNS Travel.

  • Smith Taxis contact details
  • Access Taxis contact details
  • BNS Travel contact details
  • Uber in Malvern

A Sunday walk

If you are staying in Malvern on Sunday and fancy a walk, the climb from the town centre to the top of Worcestershire Beacon is rewarding but challenging, with some steep sections.

If you are driving and would prefer something gentler, Black Hill car park on Jubilee Drive is the best shortcut. From there, a short path with a gentle gradient leads up to the ridge and panoramic views.

Reception details

Food, seating, and practical notes

Food

Welcome drinks and canapés will be served from 14:00. A buffet meal will begin from 15:00.

The buffet will feature traditional Lebanese and Moroccan cuisine, with vegetarian options available.

Some dishes may contain nuts, and we cannot guarantee that any dish will be completely free from nuts or traces of nuts. Guests with nut allergies should take particular care.

Drinks

Soft drinks, tea and coffee, as well as wine, local beer and local cider, will be available. If you have a favourite alcoholic drink that is not listed, please feel free to bring your own.

Seating

Seating at the reception will be informal. Please choose any available table, except tables marked Reserved

Children

Children are warmly invited. The reception hall has a large sports field, and there should be some balls and basic games available.

Dress code

There is no particular dress code — please wear whatever makes you feel comfortable and ready to celebrate. It is a wedding, and the ceremony takes place at Great Malvern Priory, a beautiful Church of England church, so please bear the occasion and setting in mind.

Photography

Visit the Photo Guestbook table during the reception, take an instant photo, add it to our scrapbook and leave us a message.
